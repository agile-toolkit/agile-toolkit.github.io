Network check

Team sessions in Planning Poker and Moving Motivators travel through free public relays. This page tries each one from your network — office, home or VPN — and tells you which get through.

    MQTT brokers use ports 8884, 8084 and 8081, which corporate firewalls often block. Nostr relays use the standard web port 443.

    Relays marked candidate aren’t used by the apps yet; they’re checked to see whether adding them would help.

    Each check only opens a connection and asks the relay to answer. No session data is sent. As with any website, the relays see your IP address.
